# Lumi Flair v1.4.12 — Moment Card fix 🖼️

A quick fix for Moment Cards.

## 🖼️ Portraits fit properly
The round portrait now always fills the circle, whatever the shape of the picture. Before, pictures that weren't tall enough left a gap at the top.
If the character has a square crop of their avatar (the one Lumiverse shows in round avatar spots), the card now uses it for the portrait. That way a full-scene avatar no longer gets squeezed into the circle. The full picture still makes the blurred background.

**Update:** click **Update** on Lumi Flair in Extensions, then refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R). On a home-screen app, force-close and reopen it.
