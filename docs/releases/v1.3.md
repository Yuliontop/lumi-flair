# Lumi Flair v1.3 — Living Sound 🔊

Your story can now make noise, plus a round of fixes from phone testing.

## 🎬 AI sound effects
The AI can drop short sound cues into a reply, and they play as the line appears: a door knock, creak or slam, footsteps, sword clash, glass break, heartbeat, thunder, bell, whoosh, impact, magic, splash, fire crackle. All 14 are generated live (no audio files), each has a preview button, and you can swap in your own file for any cue.
Off by default. Turn it on under **Flair → Sound → AI sound effects** (adds about 200 tokens to each request).

## 🎚️ Collapsible volume widget
Fold the floating pill into a small round button so it stops cluttering the screen. Tap it to open, drag it to move it. It starts folded on phones and remembers your choice.

## 💬 Choice chips follow swipes
Swiping now replaces the chips instead of stacking a new set on top, and swiping back brings that version's own chips back.

## 📱 Phone fixes
- Achievements are now kept across sessions
- Achievement pop-ups clear the notch on iPhone home-screen apps
- Lighter scene effects (light rays, neon, grain, particles), so phones stay smooth
- The composer's Home button no longer reloads the page

**Update:** click **Update** on Lumi Flair in Extensions, then refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R). On a home-screen app, force-close and reopen it.
