# Lumi Flair v1.6.1 — Lighter, with Smooth Motion ⚡

A performance update. Lumi Flair now uses noticeably less GPU, and there's a new switch if you'd rather keep everything at full speed.

## 🔋 Lighter by default
Lumiverse draws frosted glass behind your messages, and every time a full-screen layer redraws, the browser has to re-blur that glass. Lumi Flair had three layers doing this at your screen's full refresh rate (60, 120 or 144 times a second), which is where the GPU time went. Now:

- **Particle scenes** (snow, rain, embers, fireflies, petals, stars) are drawn at about 30 fps. They also no longer measure their canvas on every frame, which forced the page to re-layout each time.
- **The audio visualizer** is drawn at about 30 fps (about 15 in battery saver). The desktop app only sends sound data 30 times a second anyway, so extra frames showed nothing new. Its background layer is drawn at normal resolution instead of 1.5×.
- **Cinematic light** (sunbeams, neon, candle) moves in fine steps instead of every frame, as it already did on phones. The steps are too small to see.
- Battery saver's frame-rate watcher no longer runs for the light, since the light is already cheap.

## 🌊 Smooth motion ⭐ *new*
**Flair panel → General → Smooth motion.** Turn it on to draw particles, the visualizer and the light at your screen's full frame rate (60 or more) again. It looks silkier and uses more GPU and battery. It's **off by default**.

Battery saver still steps in if your machine struggles, and Smooth motion yields to it until things recover.

## 💡 Good to know
- Nothing else changed: your settings, packs, sounds and the visualizer's look are all as they were.
- If you liked the full frame rate, switch Smooth motion on once and it stays on.
- Still seeing high GPU? Film grain uses a full-screen blend effect that is one of the most expensive things Lumi Flair draws, so try turning it off first. Lumiverse's own effects use GPU too.

**Update:** click **Update** on Lumi Flair in Extensions, then refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R).
