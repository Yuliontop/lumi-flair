# Lumi Flair v1.6.1 — Lighter, with Smooth Motion ⚡

A performance update: less GPU, same look.

## 🔋 Lighter by default
Every redraw of a full-screen layer makes the browser re-blur Lumiverse's frosted glass behind your messages, and Flair was redrawing at your screen's full refresh rate. Now:
• Particle scenes and the audio visualizer draw at about **30 fps**
• The light (sunbeams, neon, candle) moves in tiny steps you can't see
• The visualizer's background layer is drawn at normal resolution

## 🌊 Smooth Motion (new)
**Flair panel → General → Smooth motion** puts all of it back at your screen's full frame rate (60+). Silkier, but uses more GPU and battery. Off by default. Battery saver still steps in if your machine struggles.

## 💡 Tip
Still high GPU? Try turning off **Film grain** first, it's one of the priciest things Flair draws.

Update: Extensions → Lumi Flair → **Update**, then Ctrl+Shift+R
