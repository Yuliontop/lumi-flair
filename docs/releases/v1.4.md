# Lumi Flair v1.4 — Center Stage 🎭

Four new ways to enjoy the story, plus better Moment Cards.

## ⭐ Favourite moments
Tap the ★ under any message (or select a line first) to pin it. Pins collect in a reel in the Flair panel, show as stars on the story heartbeat, and take you straight back to the moment. Optionally save them to Lumiverse's memory so the AI remembers them (uses your Memory Cortex; Flair adds no tokens).

## 🎬 Character intro
Opening a chat plays a short name card in the character's aura colour, with an optional theme sound from your own library. In group chats a chip shows who's speaking, in their colour, and the other messages dim while they talk.

## 🎭 Theater mode
One click hides the interface and the story fills the screen: bigger text, long replies shown in full, and a gentle auto-scroll. Atmosphere and sound carry on. Start it from the button under any message, the input bar's Extras menu or the Flair panel.

## ⌨️ Typewriter pacing
Replies flow in at a steady pace instead of in bursts, with soft key sounds that follow the character's mood (or your own sound). Off by default.

## 🖼️ Moment Cards
Wider cards, pick exactly which lines go on them, and they save like a screenshot: rounded corners and a soft shadow on a transparent background.

## 🛠️ Fixes
- Flair's buttons line up properly in the message action bar
- Flair windows no longer close when a text selection is dragged outside them

**Update:** click **Update** on Lumi Flair in Extensions, then refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R). On a home-screen app, force-close and reopen it.
