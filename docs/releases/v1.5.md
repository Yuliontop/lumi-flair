# Lumi Flair v1.5 — Cursor Trails ✨

Your mouse leaves a little magic behind it now.

## 🖱️ Cursor trail
A trail follows your pointer anywhere in Lumiverse, themed on the send effects you already know:

- 💧 **Splash**: water droplets that spray and fall
- 🍦 **Creamy**: soft drips of cream
- 🌸 **Petal Storm**: sakura petals drifting off on the wind
- 🕳️ **Black Hole**: motes that spiral into where your cursor just was
- ☄️ **Comet**: a glowing tail of sparks
- 🎉 **Confetti**: tiny paper pieces in your theme's colours

It picks up your theme colour, so it fits any Flair Pack.

## 📏 Trail length
A slider right below sets how long the trail lingers, from a quick flick to a long, lazy tail.

## 💡 Good to know
- Find it in the Flair panel under **When you send → Cursor trail**. Off by default.
- Mouse only: touch screens have no pointer to follow.
- Respects reduced motion, never flickers (safe with No flashing), goes lighter in battery saver, and rests while your mouse does.

**Update:** click **Update** on Lumi Flair in Extensions, then refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R).
